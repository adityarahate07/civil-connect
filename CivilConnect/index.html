About CivilConnect Buldhana

Digitizing construction labour, contracting, and site supervision across Vidarbha

🏗️ Our Mission

CivilConnect is built specifically for the civil engineering and construction ecosystem of Buldhana District, Maharashtra. Our platform bridges the gap between daily-wage construction labourers, skilled masons, shuttering carpenters, civil contractors, builders, and on-site supervisors.

📍 Comprehensive 13 Taluka Coverage

Whether a site requires 20 helpers in Khamgaon, foundation masons in Mehkar, heritage artisans at Sindkhed Raja, temple guest house electricians in Shegaon, or irrigation wall stone layers in Motala, CivilConnect enables instant direct contact without predatory middleman commissions.
